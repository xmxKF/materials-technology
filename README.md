# Materials & Technology

Multilingual corporate website concept for semiconductor materials, inspection equipment and solar-related business.

Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and English share reserved layout geometry. Language selection is remembered locally. GitHub Pages uses browser time-zone and language hints; it does not provide exact IP geolocation.

The site has 54 pages: seven company/experience pages, three catalog browsing pages and 44 original material, equipment and technical topic pages. Browse by product family, seven industries or fourteen applications. Search and combined filters run locally and can be shared through the URL.

Technical copy is independently written. The reference catalog informed category coverage; supplier descriptions and photographs are not reproduced. Public reference links provide background and imply no partnership. Product availability, specifications and brand representation require separate confirmation.

Images are AI-generated conceptual illustrations, not photographs of company facilities or representations of supplied product models. The 3D lab contains an interactive optical inspection prototype with rotation, part selection and an exploded view. Reconstruction quality remains under review; it is not a validated product model or engineering CAD. The ceramic ring remains a reference image pending multi-view materials.

This repository contains the public static website only. GitHub Pages publishes the `main` branch root. Relative asset URLs support the repository subpath. The company identity and contact information remain placeholders until confirmed.

The page includes repeatable scroll reveals in both directions and respects reduced-motion preferences. No sign-in is required to view this website. No analytics, external font service, or third-party geolocation request is used.

Every one of the 44 product topics has its own independently generated 3D-style conceptual image. Cards and details for the same topic share that image for recognition. The equipment overview also has a separate image. All 45 additions use optimized WebP assets.

Four user-supplied AI concept films are embedded in Home, Materials, Inspection and Technical Services. The home background loops silently and respects reduced-motion preferences. Other films use native playback controls. Public clips have no audio track; original source files remain private.

Contact details are intentionally blank pending confirmation. The multilingual contact form downloads an inquiry to the visitor’s device; it does not send messages or upload form data. The Contact page, homepage section, business CTAs and footer share clear navigation.
